A wall panel for the WARP Energy Manager: energy flow, today's and overall self-sufficiency, the solar forecast, the history chart with input/relay/SG-Ready states, the wallbox charge modes, the heating (SG-Ready) status with temperatures and a two-hour party-mode override, and the service page.

Blank panel? The installer above flashes it over USB, straight from the browser. Then the panel starts its open setup network and waits until it has joined your WLAN.

Already installed? The panel updates itself over the air and from its own web portal - use that, not the installer.

Runs on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7 (7 inch, 800x480).
